# Drive Ready – AI Agents | OPC 2026

## About

This repository documents my daily hands-on learning and practical implementations as part of the Drive Ready AI Agents – One Person Company (OPC) initiative conducted by the Department of Technical Training, Aditya University.

## Business Case Study – CampusBites

CampusBites is an AI-powered campus canteen assistant designed to support customer queries, menu recommendations, order management and business operations using Agentic AI.

The system will be developed progressively throughout the training program.
